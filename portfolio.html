About

R Agneeshwaran is a Biotechnology graduate transitioning into UI/UX design. Years spent in labs and research settings taught him to observe carefully, test methodically, and document clearly — habits that now shape how he approaches interfaces.

He's drawn to design that stays out of the user's way: clear hierarchy, honest feedback, and flows that make sense on the first try. What started as curiosity about why some apps feel effortless and others don't has turned into a deliberate shift toward user-centred design.

He's currently building a portfolio of self-initiated concept projects, learning Figma, Adobe XD, Photoshop and Illustrator, and looking for an entry-level role or internship where a research-minded, detail-oriented approach to design would be useful.
